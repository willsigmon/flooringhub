Luxury Vinyl Plank Installation

Luxury vinyl plank brings a wood-look finish to your home. Flooring Hub offers LVP installation across Raleigh, Durham, Cary and the Triangle, with material selection and installation handled directly with Tom Smith.

The service

What to consider for your project.

Wood-look flooring with resilience

Flooring Hub presents LVP as a waterproof, scratch-resistant flooring option with the appearance of hardwood. The material-selection visit lets you see samples in your own rooms alongside your furniture and lighting. Talk with Tom about your space and the options before choosing your floor.

Preparation is part of the installation

Tom assesses the space during the free in-home consultation. When a subfloor needs leveling, that work is included as installation prep; Flooring Hub does not list leveling as a separate service. Tom also handles moisture mitigation and challenging layouts, and oversees installation and cleanup personally.

See the warm-grain Raleigh installation

The gallery shows a warm-brown LVP installation in Raleigh. It is a useful starting point for discussing the grain and color you have in mind. You can also compare the hardwood and laminate examples in the gallery before reviewing material samples at home.
